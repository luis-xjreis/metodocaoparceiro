Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what data metodocaoparceiro.shop ("Partner Pup", "we") collects, why, and your choices. The data controller is Reis Latam.

1. Data we collect

  • Quiz answers: information about your dog (name, age, breed, behaviors) and your routine.
  • Contact: your email address.
  • Visit source: campaign parameters (UTM) from the link you used.
  • Purchase data: name, email and payment details are entered directly in the Cakto checkout. Payments are processed by Cakto and we never receive your card details.

2. How we use it

  • To build and show your personalized plan.
  • To email your plan summary and training tips, when you opt in.
  • To deliver the products you purchase and provide support.
  • To understand which ads and pages work best.

3. Who we share it with

Only services needed to run the site: Cakto (payments, delivery and members area), GitHub Pages (hosting) and FormSubmit (forwards your quiz contact to our email). Advertising measurement tools such as the Meta Pixel receive browsing data. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Retention

We keep data as long as needed for the purposes above or to meet legal obligations. Quiz contact data is deleted upon request.

5. Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data, or unsubscribe from emails at any time, by writing to reisdroplatamsuporte@gmail.com. California residents may exercise their CCPA rights the same way.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and ad measurement. You can block them in your browser settings.

7. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
